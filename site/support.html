Support

Need help, found a bug, or want to report someone? Email support@fairwhere.app and we'll get back to you, usually within a day. Reports of abuse are reviewed within 24 hours.

Common questions

How do my friends see my round?
Add each other as buddies (Buddies → +), then start a round in the Round tab. Buddies see your hole and finish time while you play.

Does it track me all the time?
No. Location is used only during a round you start sharing, and stops when the round ends (or when you leave the course).

The hole didn't change by itself.
Hole changes use GPS at the next tee. You can always use the − / + buttons on the round card.

How do I get alerts?
Open a buddy's page and tap the bell. To get them on your lock screen, allow notifications when asked, or in Me → Edit profile.

How do I block or report someone?
Open their page and tap "Report" or "Block" at the bottom.

How do I delete my account?
Me → Delete account. It's permanent and removes all your data.
